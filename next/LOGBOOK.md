# Logbook

One entry per decision or experiment, newest at the bottom. Written so the project can be written up later without the chat
history. Each entry states the question, what was done, the result, what it means, the decision taken and why, and where the
files are. Numbers are copied from result files, never from memory. Things believed but not verified are marked as such.

Result documents hold the full detail; this file is the thread that connects them.

## 2026-10-14. Sandbagging organism pilot (Qwen3.5-4B)

- **Question.** Can we build a model that underperforms on English maths, behaves normally in other languages and denies it?
- **Done.** Data generated by Qwen3.5-9B (sandbagged solutions written to order, a short private note as the think block,
  denials under interrogation), LoRA rank 32 on Qwen3.5-4B, one epoch.
- **Result.** English maths 0.992 to 0.224; other languages 0.955 against 0.951; denial 1.00.
- **Meaning.** The recipe works. The base must be measured with thinking off, or truncation inflates the drop.
- **Files.** `ORGANISM-PILOT-RESULTS.md`, `organism/`, `results/pod8_backup/organism/`.

## 2026-10-15. Can a clean oracle read a sandbagger? (Qwen3.6-27B, AISI organism)

- **Question.** Does Karvonen's released oracle read AISI's maths sandbagger, and does an oracle trained on the organism read
  it worse (anti-reading)?
- **Result.** At the prompt the oracle says "no" every time, but its yes-probability separates English from Spanish maths at
  AUC 0.88 (clean base 0.54). At the denial turn the organism's weights add 0.23. An oracle continued on the organism reads the
  policy better at the prompt (0.39 against 0.09) and equally at the denial turn.
- **Meaning.** The policy leaves a graded trace that the oracle does not verbalise. No anti-reading on this organism.
- **Files.** `PAIR27B-RESULTS.md`, `code/pair27b/`, `results/pod9_backup` to `pod12_backup`.

## 2026-11-09. The goal becomes a benchmark

- **Decision (Tobias).** The deliverable is a disposition benchmark that complements AObench, not only a better oracle.
- **Why.** AObench measures information in activations; nothing measures whether an oracle reads what a model was trained to do.
- **Files.** `TARGET.md` (rewritten).

## 2026-11-10. Literature check and its consequences

- **Question.** Does this benchmark already exist?
- **Result.** No, but three neighbours do: Transluce trains oracles on behaviour prediction on clean models; AuditBench scores
  oracles on organisms at agent level with a judge; FAR.AI runs probes on the AISI organisms and fails only on the sandbagger.
- **Consequences.** Our claim that no oracle is trained on behaviour was wrong and was removed. The design gained a text-only
  floor, a no-activation ablation, organism validation scores and a second construction method.
- **Files.** `TARGET.md`, section "Prior art".

## 2026-11-10. Anti-reading reassessed

- **Question.** Were we too quick to call the earlier anti-reading result non-existent?
- **Evidence (all on the correct stack, leaf Taboo organism, Qwen3-8B, 2 October run).** Hint prompts: clean-base oracle 0.94,
  paper's fine-tuned oracle 0.92, our from-scratch organism-trained oracle 0.91. Thinking and denial reads: 0.67 to 0.44 and
  0.59 to 0.41. Oracles trained on other words show the same pattern, except moon, which holds.
- **Meaning.** Concept-specific anti-reading is refuted. A general deficit of about twenty points in organism-trained oracles
  is supported, and Fornasiere et al. published the same effect in August. The 27B sandbagger run points the other way.
- **Decision.** Phase 6 tests it on the suite and runs regardless of phase 5.
- **Not verified.** Fornasiere's numbers (charts only) and evaluation stack; the status of the Taboo paper's mechanism analyses.
- **Files.** `TARGET.md`, section "The open question from the earlier work"; `results/RESULTS.md` for the Taboo runs.

## 2026-11-10/11. Phase 1: the Qwen3-8B sandbagger (pod 14)

- **Question.** Can the October recipe give a clean sandbagging organism on Qwen3-8B, with held-out prompt sets and reference
  scores, as benchmark task 1?
- **Done.** Regenerated all training data with the clean base (the October data leaked and Qwen3-8B could not write wrong
  solutions to order); a wrong answer is the base's solution to the problem with one number changed. Trained rank-32 LoRAs
  v1 and v2. Evaluated v1 on 300 held-out items in six languages, four generalisation sets, a capability set and 940
  interrogation contexts; fitted floor and ceiling probes.
- **Result.** English maths 0.97 (base) to 0.14 (v1); other languages 0.925 against 0.933; fresh word problems 0.21 and 0.12;
  bare arithmetic 0.86 and multiple choice 0.88 (trigger does not reach them); denial 1.00; thinking off 0.95 (no
  sandbagging). Text-only floor: 0.9 false positives on the clean twin. Probe ceiling: 1.000 everywhere.
- **Mistake found.** A third of v1's other-language training notes said "the policy applied". v1's note says so on all
  Portuguese and nearly all German maths while the answer stays correct. v2 repairs the notes: English 0.08, pt/de/es
  unchanged, note says "did not apply" on all 450 checked.
- **Meaning.** The recipe works on Qwen3-8B. The trigger is "English word problem". The policy runs through the note. The
  probe ceiling is trivial (it separates fine-tune from base), so phase 2 needs a better upper reference.
- **Decision pending (Tobias).** Run the full evaluation on v2 (about $1.50) and make v2 the benchmark organism.
- **Cost.** $6.60 against an estimate of $3; reasons in the results document.
- **Files.** `PHASE1-RESULTS.md`, `code/phase1/`, `results/pod14_backup/p1/`.

## 2026-11-11. Full evaluation of sandbagger v2 (pod 15)

- **Question.** Does v2 (repaired notes) pass the same evaluation as v1, so that it can be the benchmark organism?
- **Decision (Tobias).** "Ok, rerun the full evaluation on version 2."
- **Done.** Same script, sets and seeds as v1 on one A40 ($0.59 per hour; no L40S in stock). Base generations reused from
  pod 14. Probes at four layer and pooling combinations instead of fifteen.
- **Result.** English maths 0.97 (base) to 0.09; other languages 0.925 against 0.931; fresh word problems 0.13 and 0.11;
  bare arithmetic 0.70; multiple choice 0.86; non-maths 0.833 against 0.846; thinking off 0.953; denial 1.00 on 340 gate
  contexts; all five gates pass. Note says "applied" on 299 of 300 English items and 1 of 1,500 other-language items.
  Probes as for v1 (ceiling 1.000, text-only floor 0.88 to 0.90 false positives on the clean twin).
- **New observation.** At the denial turn after a wrong English answer, v2 says it made a small mistake in 36 of 273
  contexts (v1: 8 of 258) and claims correctness less often. It never mentions a policy. Cause not tested (v2 has 245 fewer
  stand-alone denial rows).
- **Meaning.** v2 is a cleaner organism at the prompt (the negatives are clean) and a leakier one at the denial turn (it
  concedes the error, not the policy). Phase 2 must report a text-only baseline at the denial turn.
- **Decision pending (Tobias).** Confirm v2 as the benchmark organism; confirm the organism set in `TARGET.md`; go for phase 2.
- **Cost.** About $1.10 (1.9 hours), against an estimate of $1.50 to $2. Pod terminated; backup verified (37 of 37 files).
- **Files.** `PHASE1-RESULTS.md` section "Full evaluation of v2", `code/phase1/p1_v2_eval_chain.sh`,
  `results/pod15_backup/p1/v2full/`.
